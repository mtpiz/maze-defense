# Preserve real-time execution pressure

Tactical Pause will allow inspection but no placement, upgrades, or Refits. Mission Speed begins at 1x, and 2x unlocks after defeating the first World boss. After that victory, 3x will be available through a one-time real-money Commander Pack; at a later Campaign milestone, the same permanent entitlement becomes purchasable with Research. Stars and Star totals cannot be bought. This keeps initial onboarding readable and timing tactically relevant while supporting faster replays after the player has demonstrated mastery.
