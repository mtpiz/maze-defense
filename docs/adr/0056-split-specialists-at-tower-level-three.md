# Split specialists at Tower Level 3

A specialist uses its shared family mechanic at Tower Levels 1 and 2, chooses one of two Specializations when upgraded to Level 3, and deepens that branch linearly at Levels 4 and 5. This gives every Blueprint a useful common form and one field upgrade before branch selection, while reserving three Levels for differentiated branch growth. The persistent Schematic requirements for reaching Levels 2 and 3 remain a separate decision.
