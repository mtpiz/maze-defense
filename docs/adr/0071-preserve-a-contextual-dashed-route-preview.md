# Preserve a contextual dashed Route Preview

The successor will preserve the live prototype's faded dashed route line during build phases. It will not replace that established language with two permanently competing route colors.

After Gliders are introduced, an Airborne route comparison appears when the next wave contains Airborne creeps, while the player holds a route toggle, or when the player selects or Refits a tower whose Air Blocking differs. Shared ground and Airborne segments remain one line, and only divergent Airborne segments gain spaced wing markers. Route lines do not remain visually prominent during combat.
