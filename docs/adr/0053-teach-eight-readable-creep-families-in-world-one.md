# Teach eight readable creep families in World One

World One will teach eight working-name creep families with one dominant role each: the baseline Drone, fast Skitter, armored Carapace, dense Broodling, Airborne Glider, self-healing Regenerator, ally-shielding Sporeguard, and Broodling-splitting Carrier. Each family will first appear alone or beside Drones before later waves combine them. Difficulty will come from composition and timing rather than hidden statistical variants pretending to be new enemies.
