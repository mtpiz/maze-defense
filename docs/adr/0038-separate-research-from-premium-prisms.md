# Separate Research from premium Prisms

Research will remain the broadly earnable soft currency, while real-money purchases provide a separate premium currency called Prisms. Selected difficult, one-time accomplishments such as Masteries may grant small fixed Prism awards, but normal play will not farm them. Prisms may buy Research, an exact Blueprint's Level 1 unlock, later level-specific Schematics or Segments, and cosmetics; they may not buy Stars, World Gates, Blueprint Ceilings, Mastery results, or Field Credits during a Mission. The store will present a Level 1 Schematic as the corresponding Blueprint unlock rather than exposing internal acquisition terminology. This supersedes ADR-0034's direct cash-to-Research model and lets the game monetize desirable Blueprints without random acquisition or a paid-exclusive power ceiling.
