# Measure voluntary continuation and strategic diversity

Initial Vertical Slice hypotheses are that at least 80 percent of uncoached testers complete Mission One without human intervention, at least 70 percent demonstrate correct Waypoint and Airborne-route understanding after Mission Three, at least 60 percent voluntarily begin another Mission after victory, and at least 35 percent voluntarily replay for a better Star Rating or Mastery within their first week.

Late-Mission testing should produce at least three meaningfully different successful maze and Loadout patterns, with no specialist appearing in more than 90 percent of eligible clean victories across the full World. Device sessions should exceed 99 percent crash-free while satisfying the relevant frame-performance mode. These are falsifiable starting hypotheses, not launch guarantees; voluntary behavior and observed understanding matter more than complimentary surveys.
