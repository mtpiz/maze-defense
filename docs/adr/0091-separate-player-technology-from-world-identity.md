# Separate player technology from World identity

The Foundation Tower and core specialist Tower Blueprints will retain one coherent science-fiction
Player Technology identity across every World because they are permanent account unlocks, not local
building sets. Worlds may use radically different environments and enemy factions without reskinning
those towers; World One is the Brood World and establishes the contrast between persistent futuristic
defenses and a biological or biomechanical infestation. A World may eventually award a locally
inspired Blueprint that is mechanically advantaged there and then joins the permanent roster, but a
one-per-World cadence is not yet a contract. This separation preserves tower recognition, makes
unlocks feel owned, and prevents every new World from multiplying the core tower asset burden.
