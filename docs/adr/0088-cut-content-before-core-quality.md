# Cut content before core quality

If schedule pressure threatens the quality bar, production will first simplify elaborate Galaxy Map animation and future-World presentation, then reduce unique Arenas from approximately eight to six through stronger rematches, then reduce the main path from ten Missions to eight while preserving both optional Trials and the boss. Live billing and rewarded-ad plumbing may become a fully testable simulated store, and cross-device sync may slip while robust local persistence and recovery remain.

The core maze, ordered Waypoints, understandable Airborne routing, four specialist families through Level 3, World boss, deterministic simulation, portrait controls, accessibility baseline, and performance standards are not scope-cut candidates. Fewer excellent Missions are preferable to recreating the prototype's repetitive quantity.
