# Keep Siege visible and Gravity hidden

World One will distinguish accessible optional progression from a three-Star secret. The Siege Trial appears as a visible branch after completing Mission Four, ensuring the free player can discover a third specialist and use its Stars as an alternate contribution toward the World Gate. The Gravity Trial's branch physically appears after earning three Stars on Mission Six.

The Blueprint catalog may hint at an unidentified signal near Mission Six so Gravity remains discoverable without spoiling the World Map reveal. This preserves a Super Mario World-style secret while avoiding the progression contradiction created when every substitute-Star Mission requires an already-perfect result.
