# Target short authored Missions

Ordinary Campaign Missions will target six to eight minutes at normal play speed for an experienced player making ordinary use of Early Launch, while a cautious first attempt may take eight to twelve minutes. Boss Missions target ten to fourteen minutes for experienced players and may reach approximately eighteen minutes when the player uses every planning interval. Missions will create their depth through consequential maze revisions, distinct threat compositions, and escalating tactical phases rather than 75–80 waves of repeated enemies.
