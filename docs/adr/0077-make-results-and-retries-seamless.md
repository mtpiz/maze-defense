# Make results and retries seamless

Victory will animate earned Stars and deterministic rewards, reveal newly opened World Map connections, and offer immediate Continue or Replay actions. Failure will identify the final wave and leaking Creep Families, then offer retry with the saved Opening Plan, opening revision, or Loadout change without prescribing one correct solution.

Neither outcome inserts a forced advertisement or automatic revive interruption. Result presentation may be celebrated, but it must be quickly skippable so forward progress and strategic retries remain nearly continuous.
