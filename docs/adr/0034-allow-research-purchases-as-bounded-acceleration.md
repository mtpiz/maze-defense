# Allow Research purchases as bounded acceleration

Research will be earnable through play and purchasable directly for real money. A player may therefore buy both a required Schematic and the Research needed to activate it, but payment cannot buy Stars, bypass the Campaign-controlled Blueprint Ceiling, or raise the final combat-power ceiling. This supersedes ADR-0011's earn-only restriction and treats paid Research as acceleration toward the same bounded account progression available to every player.
