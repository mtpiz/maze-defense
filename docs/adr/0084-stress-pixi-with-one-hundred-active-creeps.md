# Stress Pixi with one hundred active creeps

The four-week Pixi proof will raise its stress case from 50 to approximately 100 simultaneously active creeps, including Broodling density, plus roughly 40 firing towers and representative projectiles, chains, beams, shields, compact damage text, deaths, audio, and 3x simulation speed.

The quality target remains 60 frames per second on a representative three-year-old midrange Android device, measured through frame-time, memory, thermal, and battery instrumentation rather than visual impression alone. Presentation density may adapt through the Combat Readability Budget before tactical information or simulation behavior is reduced. This decision supersedes the 50-creep load in ADR 0026 while retaining its visual-quality and engine-switch criteria.
