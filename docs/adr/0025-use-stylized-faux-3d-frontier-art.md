# Use stylized faux-3D frontier art

Status: Superseded by [ADR-0091](0091-separate-player-technology-from-world-identity.md).

The first World will depict stylized frontier machinery resisting a biomechanical swarm. Authored tiles will use material, shape, and shadow to suggest depth across grass, soil, metal, and rock; towers will read as machines and creeps as organic threats. Neon color is reserved for energy, routes, attacks, status, and other gameplay signals instead of covering every surface. Strong silhouettes and battlefield readability on a portrait phone take priority over realistic runtime 3D, black wireframes, and ornamental detail.
