# Establish World One Arena grammar

World One will target a fixed 9-column by 14-row board that remains fully visible on a portrait phone without camera movement. The smallest supported phone sets the minimum usable tile and touch-target size; the grid remains a prototype target until validated on representative devices.

World One uses only open buildable floor, hard terrain that blocks both route layers, unbuildable spawn and exit tiles, and unbuildable ordered Waypoint tiles. Buff pads, hazards, destructible or moving terrain, player barricades, and layer-specific terrain blockers are reserved for later Worlds.

Arena entrances and exits are not forced into a universal top-to-bottom arrangement. Entrances may begin on the top, left, or another clearly readable location, and future Arenas may begin near the center for patterns such as a spiral. Ordered Waypoints can deliberately send creeps to different regions, reverse the broad direction of travel, and route them repeatedly past well-positioned towers.

Mission One uses no Waypoint. A later early Mission introduces the requirement that every ground and Airborne creep physically touch each visible Waypoint in order. During build phases, the game preserves the live prototype's faded dashed Route Preview through the resulting maze.
