# Author deterministic purposeful Campaign waves

Campaign Missions will use deterministic authored wave scripts, including Creep Families, entrances, counts, and timing. Retrying a Mission will therefore test learning and redesign rather than luck.

At Mission opening, the player may review the standard Wave Briefing for every wave reached in a previous attempt. Unseen waves remain represented only by the Mission Threat Forecast, and exact counts and spawn timing remain undisclosed.

Every Mission follows an establish, complicate, and test structure. Each wave must introduce, combine, reverse, or test a tactical idea; increasing health or volume alone is not a sufficient purpose. These rules do not preclude numerical tuning, but they prohibit filler waves.
