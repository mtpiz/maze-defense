# Ease players into the Campaign without a separate tutorial

The first session will move from a short skippable arrival to the mechanical World Map and Mission One, targeting meaningful interaction within seconds and combat within roughly 60–90 seconds. It will not place an account wall, store offer, notification request, rewarded advertisement, or settings questionnaire before gameplay.

There is no separate tutorial. Mission One briefly guides route reading, drag-building Foundations, installing Rail, and launching a wave before returning control. Mission Two introduces the first Waypoint, Skitters, upgrades, and Early Launch. Mission Three introduces Arc, Gliders, Air Blocking, and the contextual Airborne preview. Guidance appears only when its mechanic becomes relevant, remains replayable from Help, and may be skipped by returning players without skipping Campaign Missions.
