# Refund opening plans and discount live Dismantling

Tower construction will receive a full Field Credit refund throughout the untimed opening phase so players may explore routes without restart friction. After the first wave launches, Dismantling will return approximately 80 percent of Foundation cost and 60 percent of specialist and upgrade investment. Exact values remain balance parameters. The difference keeps live maze revision viable while making commitment meaningful and Refit preferable to repeatedly demolishing complete specialist towers.
