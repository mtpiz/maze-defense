# Give World One specialists distinct combat kits

World One will establish four specialist families with different placement demands, target profiles, and Level 3 branches rather than variations on generic damage.

- **Rail** attacks ground and Airborne creeps with slow, long-range shots that ignore most Armor. Level 2 adds through-target penetration. Lance charges devastating full-lane shots; Repeater accelerates sustained fire against one target and loses momentum when retargeting. Its Doctrines are Armored, Strongest, and Best Line.
- **Arc** chains rapid small hits among nearby ground and Airborne creeps and is weak into Armor. Level 2 adds another visible jump. Interceptor trades broad chaining for longer-range rapid attacks against Fast and Airborne targets while retaining one short jump; Storm periodically discharges across dense clusters. Its Doctrines are Airborne, Fastest, and Best Chain.
- **Siege** fires expensive, long-range, ground-only area attacks, and its tile does not block Airborne routes. Level 2 adds delayed secondary impacts. Mortar fires extreme-range barrages outside a clearly displayed minimum range; Quake replaces projectiles with radial shockwaves that cover nearby maze lanes. Its Doctrines are Densest, Frontmost, and Strongest.
- **Gravity** deals negligible damage while slowing ground and Airborne creeps and drawing Light targets together under the shared Mass and nonstacking-control rules. Level 2 adds a collapse pulse. Well creates a persistent area with repeated inward pulses; Tether strongly slows one priority creep and draws nearby Light creeps toward it. Its Doctrines are Densest, Fastest, and Frontmost.

These are behavioral contracts, not final balance values. Rail and Gravity provide alternate responses to Airborne threats so that Arc is advantaged but not a named-Blueprint requirement. Levels 4–5 will deepen these identities after the vertical slice proves Levels 1–3.
