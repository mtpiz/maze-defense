# Balance Mazing without an artificial cap

Normal Campaign Missions will impose no separate tower-count or route-length limit beyond the Arena itself. Foundation Towers retain a predictable fixed Field Credit cost, while the total Field Credit curve makes maximum route length compete directly with specialist installation and development.

The initial playtest target is for successful mature builds to invest roughly 25–40 percent of total Field Credits in Foundations. Foundation-only strategies may succeed in introductory content but must fail later Capability Checks. The target is a tuning hypothesis, not a permanent balance constant.
