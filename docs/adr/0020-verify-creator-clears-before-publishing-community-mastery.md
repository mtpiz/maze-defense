# Verify Creator Clears before publishing Community Mastery

Community creators may propose a Mastery Budget for their Arena, but the Community Mastery will be saved and published only after the creator submits a deterministic completion replay that the service successfully resimulates under the exact Arena, rules, Loadout, budget, and content version. Client self-certification is insufficient because the frozen prototype's trust-based score endpoint cannot establish that a challenge is possible or fairly completed.
