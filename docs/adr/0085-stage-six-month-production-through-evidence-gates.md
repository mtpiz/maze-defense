# Stage six-month production through evidence gates

The six-month Vertical Slice will progress through five evidence-gated phases: a four-week Pixi and Android benchmark; a first-session build with near-final Missions One through Three by roughly Week Eight; complete combat, progression, profile, and World Map systems through roughly Week Fourteen; content-complete World One through roughly Week Twenty; and balance, accessibility, recovery, device, store-proof, and closed-beta hardening through Weeks Twenty-One to Twenty-Six.

Calendar time does not pass a Production Gate. The Engine, First-Session, Strategy, Content, and Release Gates require their agreed visual, performance, comprehension, build-diversity, completeness, persistence, accessibility, and recovery evidence. Failure triggers repair, an agreed scope cut, or a technology change rather than an exception disguised as completion.
