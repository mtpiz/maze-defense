# Teach ordered pass-through Waypoints progressively

Waypoint tiles will always remain traversable and never permit tower construction. Every creep must physically touch every Waypoint in order. Contact with a later marker before it becomes the next required Waypoint has no effect; the tile does not become a wall or otherwise alter pathfinding.

Routes may revisit cells and edges across successive Waypoint segments, deliberately looping creeps through strong firing areas. The Route Preview must make repeated traversal and direction legible.

Mission One uses no Waypoint, Mission Two introduces one using Drones before adding Skitters, and Mission Three introduces two before demonstrating that Gliders obey the same Waypoint Chain. Later Arenas use approximately one to four Waypoints according to their routing purpose rather than escalating the count automatically.
