# Make World One obey the maze

Every World One creep will visibly traverse the shared Waypoint Chain, with Airborne routing differing only through its blocker mask. Teleportation, burrowing, tower destruction, skipped route segments, and comparable maze-breaking behavior are reserved for later Worlds after the player understands the foundation rules. World One difficulty instead comes from speed, armor, density, regeneration, support effects, Airborne routing, and mixed compositions.
