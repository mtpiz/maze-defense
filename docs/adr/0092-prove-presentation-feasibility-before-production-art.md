# Prove presentation feasibility before production art

The four-week Engine Gate will use coherent provisional Player Technology, Brood enemies, interface
wireframes, and representative effects to prove Pixi's rendering workflow, mobile performance, and
readability under combat density; it will not require final art-direction approval or production
polish. Representative combat and the Strategy Gate must establish tower roles, enemy contracts,
wave behavior, and build diversity before the project locks production art. After that evidence, one
complete Mission and its surrounding progression surfaces receive the visual-direction and asset-
pipeline treatment before art is multiplied across World One. This accepts temporary proxy art in
exchange for reducing rework while preserving the early option to switch to Godot if Pixi cannot
support the required presentation class sustainably.
