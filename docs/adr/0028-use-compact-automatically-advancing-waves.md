# Use compact automatically advancing waves

An ordinary Mission will contain approximately ten to fourteen deliberately composed waves. Once a wave resolves, the next will launch automatically after a four-to-six-second planning interval; the player may use Early Launch for a modest temporary Mission reward. This preserves legible threat beats and brief opportunities to revise the maze without restoring the prototype's long waits, manual-launch friction, or filler waves.
