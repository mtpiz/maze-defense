# Gate World transitions with Stars

Completing a main-path Mission will continue to unlock the next Mission within its World, but entering the next World will require both defeating the current boss and meeting a cumulative Star threshold near a two-Star average across the main path. Optional Missions provide substitute Stars, and Star totals cannot be purchased. Purchased Research may make better results easier, so free first-clear progression must award enough Research for a reasonably skilled player to reach every World Gate without payment. This supersedes ADR-0006.
