# Permit purposeful World One Arena rematches

The twelve World One Missions will use approximately eight polished Arenas. Four Arenas may receive a second authored Mission distributed across the World, including the two optional Trials; Arena reuse is not restricted to Missions Eight and Nine. Any Arena may support a rematch when changed active entrances, encounters, starting conditions, loaned Blueprints, or objectives create a materially different maze problem.

The exact Mission-to-Arena allocation remains an authoring decision. Reuse exists to let players reinterpret familiar geometry with new knowledge, not to inflate Mission count; an Arena is not repeated when only enemy statistics would differ. Every Mission also provides its own three-Star replay and Mastery, so the vertical slice need not reduce visual variety by forcing all eight Arenas into two standard Missions.
