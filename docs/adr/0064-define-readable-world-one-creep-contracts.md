# Define readable World One creep contracts

World One will teach eight consistent Creep Families rather than repeatedly reskinning health and speed curves.

- Drone establishes the Light baseline.
- Skitter is Light, very fast, and fragile.
- Carapace is slow, Heavy, and visibly Armored.
- Broodling is Light, extremely fragile, and deployed in dense groups.
- Glider is Light and Airborne.
- Regenerator is Heavy and visibly charges rapid healing only after avoiding damage briefly.
- Sporeguard is Heavy and projects a visible, nonstacking damage barrier onto nearby allies but not itself.
- Carrier is Heavy and visibly releases Broodlings at its current route position when destroyed.

Their behavioral contracts remain stable as numerical difficulty grows. Exact health, speed, barrier strength, recovery delay, spawn counts, and Mass tuning remain subject to playtesting.
