# Sell level-specific Schematics and Current Ceiling Bundles

The store will sell deterministic level-specific Schematics and may offer a discounted Current Ceiling Bundle containing every missing Schematic for that Blueprint up to the player's present Blueprint Ceiling. Owned Schematics and Segments reduce the bundle contents and price. The store will never sell future-locked Levels, random Blueprint packs, or duplicates that waste prior progress.
