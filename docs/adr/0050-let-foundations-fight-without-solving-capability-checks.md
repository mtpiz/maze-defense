# Let Foundations fight without solving Capability Checks

An unspecialized Foundation Tower will fire a weak, short-range, ground-only pulse that can handle tutorial enemies and finish stragglers. Its aggregate damage will scale poorly against armor, regeneration, Airborne threats, and later health growth, ensuring that a well-shaped Foundation-only maze cannot solve the Campaign. This preserves the familiar feel of placing towers rather than inert barricades while keeping specialist choice essential.
