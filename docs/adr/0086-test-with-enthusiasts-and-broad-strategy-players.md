# Test with enthusiasts and broad strategy players

Production will distribute weekly device builds once the mobile loop exists. The first uncoached Mission One-through-Three study will use approximately 12–20 players divided between tower-defense enthusiasts and broader mobile strategy players. A mid-production alpha may expand to roughly 40–75 players, followed by a 200–500-player Android closed beta only after earlier Gates pass.

Friends may participate but cannot be the only usability evidence. With informed consent, testing records anonymized commands, maze layouts, retries, leaks, tower usage, frame times, device class, and crashes. Enthusiasts expose shallow strategy while newcomers expose incomprehensible presentation.
