# Use offline-first cloud-recoverable Player Profiles

The Campaign will remain playable offline after its assets are installed. First launch creates a versioned anonymous local Player Profile without an account prompt. Optional Google or Apple linking later enables cloud recovery and cross-device synchronization; Stars and permanent unlocks merge monotonically.

Research, Prisms, reward claims, purchases, and entitlements use an auditable Economy Ledger rather than trusting mutable client totals. Premium transactions are server-validated, and ranked or Community submissions require a synchronized profile. Real-money testing cannot begin until automated migration, corruption recovery, purchase restoration, and conflict tests pass.
