# Budget combat feedback for accessibility and performance

The Vertical Slice will ship to closed testing with color-independent gameplay signals, photosensitivity-safe defaults, reduced-flash and reduced-shake options, an effect-intensity control, independent music, effects, and haptic controls, readable UI scaling, high-contrast route and range presentation, and explicit 30 FPS battery and 60 FPS quality modes.

[The Tower - Idle Tower Defense](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.TechTreeGames.TheTower) is a presentation benchmark for continuous mobile action, compact damage numbers, and perceived smoothness, not for idle progression or monetization. Damage text and effects operate within a Combat Readability Budget: high-frequency events may be pooled, abbreviated, sampled, or aggregated instead of creating one persistent visual for every hit. Tactical state and frame stability take precedence over spectacle.
