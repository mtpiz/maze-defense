# Teach World One through a ten-Mission curriculum

World One will introduce one primary Creep Family or tactical concept through each of its first eight main Missions, then test synthesis rather than adding more rules.

1. Drones teach basic Mazing, Foundations, and the starting Rail Blueprint on an Arena without a Waypoint.
2. Skitters test route length and firing coverage, then award Arc.
3. Gliders teach how the already shared Waypoint Chain interacts with the Airborne Route.
4. Carapaces teach Armor and open the optional Siege unlock Mission.
5. Broodlings teach dense waves and multiple active entrances.
6. Regenerators teach continuous coverage and open the optional Gravity Trial.
7. Sporeguards teach support formations and target priority.
8. Carriers teach death-spawn timing and exit risk.
9. A combined gauntlet tests demanding compositions without a new Family.
10. The Brood Matriarch serves as the World boss and full-system exam.

The Brood Matriarch begins slow and Armored, then sheds shell at two visible health thresholds, releases Broodlings, loses Armor, and gains speed. Deterministic escorts use previously taught Families. It introduces no route breaking or tower destruction. The exact thresholds, phase statistics, and escort script remain playtest variables.
