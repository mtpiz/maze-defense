Simulated events at the game's 30 Hz tick: six Foundations, three Rails, two Arcs, a Siege, Broodling swarms, and one leak. They run through the same director the game uses, which merges repeats and enforces each sound's minimum spacing.

How these were made

Rail borrows the Star Wars blaster trick: Ben Burtt struck a tensioned radio-tower guy wire, and the cable carried high frequencies faster than low ones, so the hit arrives as a falling "pew." Here that is three detuned sawtooth chirps sweeping from about 4 kHz down to 200 Hz over a metallic ring.

The wave alarm and the ambient events are modeled on real plasma-wave recordings from spacecraft: whistlers fall in pitch as lightning energy disperses along magnetic field lines, and "chorus" is a burst of rising chirps from Earth's radiation belts.

Each sound is rendered once into a buffer when audio starts, so the game only plays buffers during combat. Total bank size is about 16 seconds of mono audio and costs nothing to download.
